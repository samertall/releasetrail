What happens

1. Adena opens a wallet-connection request for this site.

2. We check the connected account equals your expected public address and the chain is onyx-1.

3. Clicking Deploy sends one /vm.m_addpkg request to Adena for your approval.

4. You review and approve the transaction in Adena. Reject it if the network, fee or destination differs.

5. We display the transaction hash; verify it succeeded and that the realm is accessible before submitting to DoraHacks.
